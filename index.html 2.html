🌍 Foreign Languages

Raise Globally
Fluent Children!

Give your children the gift of global communication. EduMama supports six international languages with age-appropriate lesson plans, reading packs, and audio resources — all alongside your Nigerian mother tongue.

🇫🇷
French
Français
300M+ Speakers

French is the second most widely spoken language in Africa, spoken in many neighbouring countries including Benin, Niger, Cameroon, and Chad. A vital second language for Nigerian children.

Nursery – SS3 Audio Lessons Printable Packs WAEC French Prep
Sample Phrase — Primary 1

🇸🇦
Arabic
العربية
420M+ Speakers

Arabic is widely spoken and studied in Northern Nigeria, and is the language of Islamic religious studies across the Muslim community. Essential for many Nigerian families.

Arabic Script Quranic Arabic Modern Standard Islamic Studies
Sample Phrase — Nursery
مرحباً! اسمي أميرة
"Hello! My name is Amira" — introduces Arabic script early
🇨🇳
Mandarin
普通话
1B+ Speakers

Mandarin Chinese is the world's most spoken language by native speakers. With growing Chinese business presence in Nigeria, Mandarin opens enormous career and trade opportunities.

Pinyin System Basic Characters Business Chinese Audio Tones
Sample Phrase — Primary 2
你好！我叫 Chukwuemeka
"Hello! My name is Chukwuemeka" — mixing cultures!
🇪🇸
Spanish
Español
500M+ Speakers

Spanish is the second most spoken language in the world by native speakers. It is relatively easy to learn for English speakers and opens doors across Latin America, Europe, and the USA.

Beginner – Advanced Songs & Rhymes Story Books Grammar Packs

Deutsch
100M+ Speakers

Germany is one of the strongest economies in the world and a top destination for Nigerian students pursuing higher education. Learning German early gives your child a major head start.

University Prep Goethe Aligned Vocabulary Packs Pronunciation Guide

260M+ Speakers

Portuguese connects Nigeria to Brazil (a major trading partner) and neighbouring African countries like Angola, Mozambique, and São Tomé. A valuable language for Nigerian business and culture.
